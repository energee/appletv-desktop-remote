Apple TV Remote

Your Apple TV, one click away in the menu bar.

Download View the source

Free for macOS and Windows.

Everything the Siri Remote does. Nothing to pick up.

Touchpad

Swipe with two fingers to move around, and click to select. Same as the remote on your couch.

Now playing

See what's on, then play, pause, and scrub from the seek bar.

Always handy

Press ⌘⇧R to show or hide it from any app. Keep it on top, or let it tuck away.

Or just use the keyboard.

Hold Option (Alt on Windows) and the remote labels every button with its key.
