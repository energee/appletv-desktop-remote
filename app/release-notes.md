## A tidier remote

- **Apps** now opens a menu of the Apple TV's apps under its icon. Pick one to launch it.
- **Keyboard** shows a text field under the remote, so the touchpad stays free while you type. It appears on its own when the TV asks for text.
- The remote no longer keeps showing something that stopped playing. A paused item clears once it has finished or has been paused for five minutes, and returns when playback resumes.
- The header is quieter: a smaller device picker, and a steady amber dot marks a sleeping TV.
- The remote stays in place and dims while it connects or when the TV is unreachable, with one status line saying what is happening.
- Hovering a button now shows its name and key.
- The pairing screen names the TV, and "Retry saving" only appears when a save failed.
- Settings has **Back** at the top and closes with Esc.

## Updating from v0.7.6 or earlier on a Mac

Pair your Apple TV again after updating. Since v0.7.7 the Mac app no longer uses Keychain, so it never asks for your password, and pairings from earlier versions don't carry over.

## Installation caveats

The Mac app is not notarized. macOS may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** for its first launch. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Your computer and Apple TV must be on the same network.
