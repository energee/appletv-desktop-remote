## What's playing, shown right

- Netflix and other apps that don't share a title now show by name, such as "Netflix", instead of "Nothing playing".
- Artwork no longer stays one selection behind in Plex. When you pick something new, its picture follows a few seconds later, once Plex provides it.

## Updating from v0.7.6 or earlier on a Mac

Pair your Apple TV again after updating. Since v0.7.7 the Mac app no longer uses Keychain, so it never asks for your password, and pairings from earlier versions don't carry over.

## Installation caveats

The Mac app is not notarized. macOS may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** for its first launch. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Your computer and Apple TV must be on the same network.
